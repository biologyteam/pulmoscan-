Values are the stored output of the training script (training/train_audio.py), unchanged, from a test set split by patient. The models were trained on the public ICBHI recordings, not on our own stethoscope. They label wheeze-like and crackle-like sounds, not asthma. An ROC-AUC near 0.5 is chance level.

How to read this

  • The decision threshold of each model was tuned on validation data to catch more positives, which lowers precision. That is why the scores in your sessions are compared with a threshold, not with 50%.
  • These results are weak. They are suitable for exploring the prototype, not for any clinical use.
  • Performance on recordings from our own stethoscope has not been measured yet.
The electronic nose has no model, so it has no performance figures. It will be evaluated separately on our own labelled data once that has been collected.
