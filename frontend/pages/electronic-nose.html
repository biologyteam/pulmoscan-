The ESP32 sends its readings to the server, so “connected” means the server has heard from it. Receiving Data: a valid reading in the last 10 s. Connected: heard from within 60 s but not sending continuously. Disconnected: nothing valid received recently. Error: the last post from the device was rejected.

Only values sent by the hardware are shown. A sensor the device does not report is marked “Not reporting”. Gas sensor values are raw device output: no unit or gas concentration is assumed.

No classification, score or probability is shown from the gas sensors. MQ-series sensors are general gas sensors, not tools for diagnosing asthma or measuring FeNO. A gas model will only be added after it is trained on our own labelled dataset and passes validation.
