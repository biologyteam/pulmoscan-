Audio Analysis

Record respiratory sounds from the stethoscope, view the waveform, and send the recording to the wheeze and crackle models.

1. Audio input

Connect the digital stethoscope (or its audio interface) to the laptop, then check that the browser can use it. Echo cancellation, noise suppression and automatic gain are switched off so the recording stays as close to the raw signal as possible.

Press “Check audio input”. The browser will ask for permission to use the input.

2. Record respiratory sound

Live waveform

Hold the stethoscope in place and breathe normally. Record at least 1 second; recording stops automatically at 30 seconds, the longest part the models analyze.

Wheeze and crackle results are two separate acoustic analyses. They are not an asthma diagnosis and not an asthma probability, and detecting either pattern does not confirm asthma. They are not combined with the electronic-nose readings. See the Research page for the objective and the limits of these models.
